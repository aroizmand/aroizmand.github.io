Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

WilderXanderr Analytics ("the app") is a personal analytics tool with a single user: the owner of the WilderXanderr YouTube channel and Instagram account. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data the app accesses

  • With the owner's permission, read-only access to his own YouTube channel through Google's YouTube Data API (youtube.readonly) and YouTube Analytics API (yt-analytics.readonly): channel and video statistics, video titles and descriptions, and aggregated, anonymous audience reports (for example views by country or traffic source).
  • The app does not access other people's accounts, and it never posts, edits or deletes content.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is stored only on the owner's personal computer and is used only to show him statistics about his own channel.
  • Video titles, descriptions and aggregated statistics may be sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate content tags and written summaries for the owner. No other third party receives the data.
  • Data is not sold, shared for advertising, or used for any purpose other than the owner's own analytics.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The owner can delete the stored data at any time by deleting the app's local database. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
